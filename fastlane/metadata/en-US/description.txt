Lug'atchi is a free, fully offline Uzbek-English-Russian dictionary. No internet connection, no account, no ads.

FEATURES
- Trilingual lookup: Uzbek ⇄ English ⇄ Russian
- Instant, typo-tolerant full-text search
- Works with both Latin and Cyrillic Uzbek script — search "o'zbek" or "ўзбек", results match either way
- Favorites and search history, stored only on your device
- Light and dark themes, adjustable font size
- Entire dictionary is bundled in the app — works with airplane mode on

Lug'atchi aggregates multiple open dictionary sources into a single searchable database, so a single search shows you headwords, part of speech, definitions in both English and Russian, and example sentences where available.

Your privacy: Lug'atchi does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything — history, favorites, settings — stays on your device. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no ads.
